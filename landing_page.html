How responses are auto-saved to Google Sheets

Google Forms features built-in instant synchronization with Google Sheets:

  • Enter Key Submission: Pressing Enter in any required text field will automatically trigger the submit function once all fields are complete.
  • Instant Row Appending: Every submission immediately creates a new timestamped row in your linked Google Sheet.
  • Real-time Notifications: You can enable email alerts under Google Forms > Responses > Get email notifications for new responses.
